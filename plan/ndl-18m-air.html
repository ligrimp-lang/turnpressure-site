NDL at 18 m on air

Turn Pressure — dive planner & gas calculator

A standard reef dive: 18 m, air, an aluminium 80, thirds as the gas strategy, calm water. Here is the full plan Turn Pressure prints for it.

The figures

  • NDL limit: 43 minutes (Bühlmann ZH-L16C, gradient factors 40/85 — the app's default).
  • Minimum gas: 57 bar.
  • Turn pressure (thirds): 157 bar, reached at minute 10.2 of the dive.
  • Time at maximum depth as planned: 12.14 minutes, for a total dive time of 27.29 minutes including the ascent ladder and safety stop.

Inputs used

  • Depth: 18 m
  • Gas: air
  • Cylinder: single aluminium 80 (11.1 L, 207 bar), started at 207 bar
  • SAC rate: 20 L/min
  • Gas strategy: thirds
  • Conditions: calm
  • Limit style: NDL with safety stop
  • Gradient factors: 40/85 (the app's default)

The MOD line for air on this plan reads 56 m — the depth the oxygen fraction actually allows, not a number capped to the app's 40 m recreational envelope. The envelope, not the gas, is what stops the dive going deeper.

Source: Turn Pressure's pinned etalon suite, case 3 — "Standard reef dive" (EtalonProfilesTests.swift), one of the 28 dives derived independently and checked on every build.
