Minimum gas from 40 m

Turn Pressure — dive planner & gas calculator

40 m is close to the deep end of what a recreational team plans. The reserve two divers need to reach the surface from there is bigger than the 30 m figure, and the gap between cylinders is bigger too.

The figures

  • 15-litre steel cylinder: 87 bar.
  • Aluminium 80 (11.1-litre): 117 bar.

Against the 50 bar habit most divers were taught, the aluminium 80 asks for more than double.

Inputs used

  • Depth: 40 m
  • Ascent style: NDL with safety stop (9 m/min to 5 m, 3 minutes at 5 m, then to the surface)
  • Cylinders: single 15-litre steel (232 bar) and single aluminium 80 / 11.1-litre (207 bar)
  • Two divers sharing one supply, at the reserve's own ascent rate

A 15-litre steel at 232 bar and an aluminium 80 at 207 bar both hold enough to fund this reserve on their own — the question the app answers is how much of the fill it costs you, which is why the cylinder you dive changes the number your dive plan is built around.

Source: the app's own pinned test suite for the figures it is sold on (PublishedReserveFiguresTests.fortyMetres).
