NDL and turn pressure at 24 m on EAN32

Turn Pressure — dive planner & gas calculator

A wreck at 24 m, planned on EAN32 (32% oxygen) in a current, on a single steel 12. Richer nitrox buys more no-decompression time than air at the same depth — here is what it buys on this dive.

The figures

  • NDL limit: 35 minutes (Bühlmann ZH-L16C, gradient factors 40/85).
  • Minimum gas: 50 bar (at an SCR of 18 L/min in a current — the reserve now scales with your own breathing rate, not a fixed one).
  • Turn pressure (thirds): 172 bar, reached at minute 10.4 of the dive.
  • Time at maximum depth as planned: 11.45 minutes, for a total dive time of 28.81 minutes including the ascent levels and safety stop.

Inputs used

  • Depth: 24 m
  • Gas: EAN32
  • Cylinder: single steel 12 L (232 bar), started at 232 bar
  • SAC rate: 18 L/min
  • Gas strategy: thirds
  • Conditions: current (raises consumption above the calm-water rate)
  • Limit style: NDL with safety stop
  • Gradient factors: 40/85 (the app's default)

MOD for EAN32 at the app's ppO2 1.4 hard block is 33 m, well past this dive's 24 m — the mix is not the limiting factor here, the NDL is.
